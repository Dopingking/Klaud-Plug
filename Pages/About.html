Our Story

Founded in 2015, KlaudPlug began as a small repair shop with a passion for technology.

What started as a single workbench for fixing laptops has grown into a full-service tech hub offering sales, repairs, and support for all your gadget needs. Our team of certified technicians has helped over 10,000 customers get the most from their devices.

We believe in quality service, honest advice, and building lasting relationships with our customers. Whether you need a new laptop, copier repair, or just tech advice, we're here to help.
